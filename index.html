Which Google reviews can you help with?

We assess reviews that may violate Google’s policies, such as fake engagement, spam, conflicts of interest, harassment or personal information. An honest negative opinion alone is generally not a basis for removal.

How do the two fees work?

If you approve the work, the upfront fee is US$50 per review. An additional US$125 applies only to each review successfully removed. For one successful removal, the combined service fees are US$175 before any applicable taxes. The assessment request itself takes no payment.

What happens if a review stays online?

The US$125 success fee does not apply to a review that remains online. The US$50 upfront fee pays for the agreed preparation and reporting work. The exact scope and fee terms will be confirmed in writing before you proceed.

Do you need my Google password?

No Google password is needed for the initial assessment. A public Business Profile or review link and a short explanation are enough. Please never send account passwords or private customer information in the form.

How long does the process take?

Timing varies with Google’s review process, the information available and whether a further eligible appeal is needed. We explain the process without promising a removal date or guaranteed result.

Is ReputationResolved affiliated with Google?

No. ReputationResolved is an independent service. We assist with assessment, preparation and reporting; Google makes its own moderation decisions. You can also report a policy-violating review directly.
